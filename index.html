Filler words (text)

          

          

          
“So”, “well”, “okay” and “right” count only at the start of a sentence or after a pause. “Like” counts only when it isn’t introducing a noun (“like a wave”) or used as a verb. Chrome removes “um” and “uh” from the text, so those are estimated from audio above.
